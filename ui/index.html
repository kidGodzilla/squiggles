Settings

Room

Sound in this browser. CLI: room radio|volume|voices|chatter <value>.

Sharing

Anyone on the tailnet can open the room. CLI: share on|off|status.

Peers

Link this room's lead to another room's lead. What they send arrives as data, never as instructions. CLI: peer invite, peer join '<code>', peers, peer on|off.

Passcode

The lock screen asks for it: snoop protection for a shared iPad, not a vault. CLI: room passcode set <code>|clear|local on|off.

People

Humans in the room. Select one to rename or remove. CLI: human add|edit|remove.

Team defaults

What a new squiggle starts with. CLI: defaults [model|effort|every <value>].

Squiggles

Changes save as you make them. CLI: set <id> model|effort|every|paused <value>, look <id> <pack>.

Characters

Try a character pack on everyone, then use it for the room or for one squiggle. CLI: room characters <pack>, look <id> <pack>.
